This blog runs on **m3blog**, a small static blog engine styled with [Material 3 Expressive](https://m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-expressive).

Everything you see — colors, corner radii, the shapes, the springy motion — is generated at build time from a handful of settings in `m3blog.config.json`.

## Make it yours

1. Change `title`, `description` and `author` in `m3blog.config.json`.
2. Pick a `theme.seedColor`. The whole palette, light and dark, is derived from it.
3. Replace this page by editing `content/pages/about.md`.
